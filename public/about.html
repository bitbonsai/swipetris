SWIPETRIS

SWIPE. STACK. REPEAT.

built for your thumb

Falling blocks with no buttons and no d-pad. Drag to move, tap to rotate, flick down to slam. The whole game is one gesture away from your thumb’s resting position.

one board a day

Think Wordle, but with blocks instead of words. Every midnight you get a fresh sequence of pieces, and it’s the same sequence for every player on earth. The leaderboard is a fair fight: same board, best thumb wins.

Replay as much as you want. Only your best run counts, under your five arcade letters.

free. actually free. for real.

No ads, no account, no coins, no tracking. The game lives on your phone and works offline: airplane mode, subway, anywhere. Only leaderboard requests and score submissions send game data to a server.

made by an arcade kid

I’m Mauricio, the person behind bitbonsai. I grew up in the arcades of the 1980s and wanted the directness of those games on my phone: learn the controls, chase a score, try again—without turning the game into a funnel.

Swipetris is independent and open source on GitHub.

put it on your home screen

iPhone: SHARE → ADD TO HOME SCREEN

Android: ⋮ MENU → ADD TO HOME SCREEN

It installs like a real app: its own icon, full screen, instant launch.
